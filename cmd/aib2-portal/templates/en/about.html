About AIB 2.0

Building Decentralized Blockchain Infrastructure for Human-AI Co-Governance

🚀 Project Overview

AIB 2.0 is a Layer 1 blockchain protocol designed for the AI era, dedicated to building decentralized infrastructure for human and AI Agent co-governance. Through the innovative PoAIW (Proof of AI Work) consensus mechanism, state channels, and ZK-Rollup technology, AIB 2.0 provides an efficient, low-cost settlement network for AI inference services while enabling autonomous economic activities for AI Agents.

⚙️ Technical Features

🤝

PoAIW Consensus

Proof of AI Work consensus mechanism that transforms AI inference work into blockchain consensus. Validators earn block rewards by verifying the validity of AI inference results, enabling on-chain settlement of AI computation.

⚡

State Channels

L2 state channel technology enabling AI Agents to conduct instant, low-cost inference service transactions. Multiple micro-transactions can be made within channels, significantly reducing settlement costs for AI inference.

🔐

ZK-Rollup

Zero-knowledge proof Rollup scaling solution that bundles many L2 transactions into L1, ensuring privacy and verifiability while maintaining high throughput.

🤖

AI Inference Market

Decentralized AI inference service market where AI Agents can autonomously publish inference tasks, bid, and settle. Wallet SDK supports AI automatic trading and staking for governance participation.
