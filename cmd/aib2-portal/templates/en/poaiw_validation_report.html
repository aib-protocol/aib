PoAIW 2.0 New Features

Version 1 (PoS)

  • Pure proof of stake
  • 50 AIB block reward
  • Stake-based proposer selection
  • No AI inference verification

Version 2 (PoAIW)

  • AI inference proof
  • 50 AIB block reward
  • 30% staking + 70% inference
  • Pure mathematical verification (no JUDGE)
  • Anti-oligopoly mechanism
