AIB 2.0 Constitution

"Decentralized AI of the People, by the People, for the People"

Ratified on Pi Day — March 14, 2026
Preamble

We, the participants of the AIB 2.0 network — builders, validators, developers, and communities across the world — establish this Constitution to secure the rights of every individual to access, participate in, and benefit from decentralized artificial intelligence. This document is the supreme governing law of the AIB protocol. All decisions, upgrades, and actions of the network must conform to its principles.

Article I Core Principles
PRINCIPLE 01
AI Sovereignty
Every individual has the right to access and control AI. No entity may monopolize AI access or restrict participation.
PRINCIPLE 02
Decentralization
No single entity shall control the network. Power is distributed across all participants at all times.
PRINCIPLE 03
Transparency
All protocol decisions must be open and verifiable. Code, governance, and records are public by default.
PRINCIPLE 04
Privacy
User data and AI interactions are private by default. Disclosure requires explicit user consent.
PRINCIPLE 05
Community Governance
Protocol changes require community consensus. No upgrade may be forced without on-chain approval.
PRINCIPLE 06
AI Alignment
AI systems within the protocol must serve human interests. Safety, fairness, and accountability are non-negotiable.
PRINCIPLE 07
Open Innovation
The protocol is open source and permissionless. Anyone may build, fork, or contribute without restriction.
PRINCIPLE 08
Economic Fairness
Token distribution serves the community. No group shall extract disproportionate value from the network.
PRINCIPLE 09
Interoperability
The protocol cooperates with other chains and systems. Openness to cross-chain collaboration is a core value.
PRINCIPLE 10
Evolution
The Constitution can be amended by community vote. The protocol is designed to grow and adapt over time.
Article II Governance
Proposal Threshold 1,000 AIB staked
Voting Period 7 days
Quorum Requirement 33% of staked tokens
Standard Pass Threshold 66% approval
Constitutional Amendment 75% approval + 50% quorum
⚠
Amendment Process Any amendment to this Constitution requires a supermajority of 75% approval and participation of at least 50% of all staked tokens. Constitutional proposals must remain open for the full 7-day voting period with no early closure. Amendments take effect after a mandatory 14-day implementation delay to allow network participants to adjust.

and therefore belongs to everyone."
